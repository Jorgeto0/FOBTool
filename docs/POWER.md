# Power Sheet

**KiCad source:** [`POWER.kicad_sch`](../POWER.kicad_sch)

The Power sheet contains the board's power-management circuitry. Its purpose is to accept and manage the available power sources, charge and monitor the single-cell battery, generate the regulated supply rails required by the system, and provide the related protection, control, and test connections.

This sheet supplies the rest of the schematic hierarchy and should be reviewed together with the power requirements of every connected subsystem.
